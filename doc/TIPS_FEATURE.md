# Tip of the Day

XeFM shows a short tip about one of its features when it starts — at most once
per day. The rotation opens with a Welcome tip introducing the dual-pane model
and pointing at the Help dialog, then steps through the rest of the features one
launch at a time: search, filtering, favorites, archives, comparing, remote
file systems, and more.

You can also open it anytime from **Help ▸ Tip of the Day…** in the menu bar.

## Usage

- **←/→** — previous / next tip (the position counter shows where you are)
- **↑/↓**, **Page Up/Down** — scroll, if a tip overflows a small window
- **Space** — toggle *Don't show tips at startup* (clicking the checkbox works
  too)
- **Enter** or **Escape** — close (clicking outside the dialog also closes it)

## Don't show again

Check **Don't show tips at startup** and XeFM stops opening the dialog on
launch. Nothing is lost: **Help ▸ Tip of the Day…** still opens it on demand,
and unchecking the box there turns the startup tips back on.

## What XeFM remembers

- Tips you have already seen are not repeated: the rotation resumes at the next
  unseen tip, and browsing ahead with **→** counts as seeing them.
- The dialog opens at startup at most once per calendar day, no matter how many
  times you launch XeFM.

Key names shown in the tips come from your live key bindings, so if you rebind
an action in `~/.xefm/config.py`, the tips quote *your* keys.
